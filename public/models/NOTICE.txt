Editor reference and original 64x32 monochrome drawing template:
PonySkincraft — http://ponyskincraft.com/skin.php?action=edit
Retrieved 12 September 2026 at the project owner's request.
The template is converted to the current 64x64 Mine Little Pony layout,
with mirrored left limbs/wings and current race/size metadata.
No authorship or additional license is claimed for the original template.
